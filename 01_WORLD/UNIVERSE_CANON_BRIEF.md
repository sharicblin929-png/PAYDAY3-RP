# PAYDAY UNIVERSE — short cross-game canon guardrails

**When to read:** only if a scene invokes franchise history, crew members, official antagonists, Shade/Bain, or an established heist. **Not** part of every-GM load. Character styles [CANON_REGISTER](CHARACTER_CANON_REGISTER.md); actor knowledge [KNOWLEDGE_LEDGER](NPC_KNOWLEDGE_LEDGER.md).

1. **PAYDAY: The Heist** (released 2011, original series): original veterans **Dallas, Chains, Wolf, original Hoxton** and heist coordinator **Bain**. Notable classic bank jobs, including **First World Bank**, underpin public reputation and later heists. The release year is not a precise diegetic calendar. Official: https://www.starbreeze.com/our-work/payday-the-heist/
2. **PAYDAY 2** (released 2013 onward): larger crew, more contractors and long-running criminal network. **Original Hoxton and Houston** are different people; Houston is Dallas's brother and for a time used the Hoxton name/mask. Bain is a pivotal organizer. Contractors/antagonists include **Vlad, Hector, The Dentist, Locke**, distinct alliances and betrayals; don't confuse their roles. Late storyline involves **Bain and the White House heist**. **Secret ending / President-Bain interpretations and retirement epilogues are disputed where later games conflict**; do not assert fan readings as unequivocal official canon. Official FBI Files are **police intelligence, not infallible narrator**: https://fbi.paydaythegame.com/ ; secondary disputed timeline https://payday.fandom.com/wiki/Timeline .
3. **New PAYDAY 2 "Under the Hammer"** was released 2026-09-15 **but is officially set before the White House job**. This is proof that real-world DLC dates do not set in-world chronology. Source: https://www.paydaythegame.com/payday2/updates/underthehammer/
4. **PAYDAY 3 original** (released 2023) concerns regrouping after a hiatus/crisis. **Shade** is important as coordinator, and some former heisters are absent or return later. **PAYDAY 3 Update 3.9 (Sep 2026) rewrote the campaign framing across existing heists**, establishing Shade, absences, and new motivations; use **the new 3.9+ framing**, not a frozen 2023 guide. Official: https://www.paydaythegame.com/news/payday3/2026/09/update-3-9/
5. Newer official stories **Boys in Blue, Houston Breakout, Fear & Greed**, extra characters, the First World Bank return and others are possible future plot; **not automatically played, not automatically known to Circle**. "RIP, John Concord" in official 3.9 notes must not be read as an event already established in OUR independent campaign.

**Guardrails:** RPG [date override](../00_SYSTEM/CHRONOLOGY_2026.md) sets **our** present to 2026-09-27; PDTH/PD2 history and real patch years unchanged. Circle **22**, not eyewitness to every historical heist. Never grant secret gang knowledge to him or another NPC without a witnessed conversation or other established channel. [KNOWLEDGE_LEDGER](NPC_KNOWLEDGE_LEDGER.md) overrides speculation about what people know. Our RPG's prohibition on deaths and total heist failures applies to **new scenes**, not an automatic rewrite of all past franchise lore.
